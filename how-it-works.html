Jobs & careers

FoundrySphere separates job-seeker journeys from company/business membership. Employers can use supported job-vacancy workflows; job seekers can explore opportunities and alerts according to their access.

Job seekers should share only information needed for their application and review privacy choices before making a profile or resume available.

🧑‍🏭

Candidate privacy is important

Personal contact details and application information should be handled according to the Privacy Policy and actual visibility controls in the service.

Business opportunities

The platform includes a guided pathway for industry-related business opportunities, allowing participants to describe the opportunity, relevant products or services, location and other applicable details.

Events, announcements & industry updates

FoundrySphere may present relevant event information, announcements and industry updates through its available workflows. Inclusion does not mean that an event or third-party claim is independently endorsed.

Feature availability may change as the service develops. This website is informational and does not claim that every described workflow is live or available to every account.
