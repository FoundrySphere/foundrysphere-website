About us

Connecting the foundry world—one useful connection at a time.

FoundrySphere is being built as an India-focused, WhatsApp-first B2B network for foundries, casting, and related industries, connecting people, products, services and opportunities.

Connect on WhatsApp ↗How it works

Our purpose

Industrial discovery can be fragmented across messages, contacts and disconnected sources. FoundrySphere aims to make relevant information and industry connections easier to discover through a guided, conversational experience.

Rather than asking people to learn another complex platform, FoundrySphere uses WhatsApp as the primary interaction channel, with a website that clearly explains the network and its membership options.
